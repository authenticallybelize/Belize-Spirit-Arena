# Story mode ideas — creator-review draft

These are proposals, not new canon or implemented missions. The current twenty-wave Chapter I remains authoritative in [STORY-CHAPTER-I.md](STORY-CHAPTER-I.md).

## Recommended direction: The Missing Memory

Keep Story focused on restoring Guardians and finding evidence. Survival supplies the long combat endurance test; Story should add a clear purpose, a new interaction and one revelation to each five-wave trial.

Chapter I ends at home with four restored doors and eight dark symbols. The next chapter can restore doors five through eight while asking a more specific question: **why were some Guardian memories removed while others survived?** Reveal an intentional pattern in what was taken, while leaving the Heart's breaker and the Jaguar's identity unresolved.

## First: strengthen Chapter I's pacing

The engine already has protective crystals, rockfalls, caves, drums, Guardian scenes, saved doorways and explicit objective text. Reuse those features.

- Add a brief practice beat before each new objective: one ordinary crystal before the Keeper's shield, one clearly marked rockfall before the Jaguar, one safe drum charge before the Warden's barrier. Reuse existing encounters rather than adding extra mandatory waves.
- Give each trial a visible restoration payoff: a small existing ground-restoration effect, a quieter soundscape and one brief Guardian reaction. Keep attack warnings readable over the scenery.
- Introduce the new wildlife through authored encounters: a grasshopper's warned leap in the Jungle, a Monkey guarding a crystal lane, a Grackle near the Pine Ridge caves and a Toad near the Coast's safe sand. These are proposed placements, not additions to the current roster.
- Offer one optional question after each purification. Optional dialogue can add personality without hiding weapons or mandatory clues behind a choice.
- Keep failure recovery at the saved wave. Explain that crystals/drums reset when retrying; retain the player's existing save protection and accessibility controls.

## Character beats

**Joe:** he begins by wanting a name to blame, then learns to compare evidence and help a damaged witness. Keep his concern practical: the door failures could reach Grandma's home.

**Grandma:** a short morning conversation can ground the stakes without revealing secret knowledge or making her a villain. Let her notice Joe's changed behavior. Any deeper link to the Heart is a separate creator decision.

**Tata:** use him as Joe's familiar, teasing guide. He knows Joe; that does not mean he knows every Guardian's missing memory.

**Hollow Keeper:** continues to recognize the fragment rather than Joe. Its missing memory motivates the investigation; do not give it a convenient full explanation between chapters.

**Jaguar:** retain recognition through gesture and a partial vision. Keep its exact identity open until the original RPG timeline is reconciled.

## Smallest useful next implementation

Build **only the Ceiba Grove trial** as a separate Story test after its story role is approved: one existing arena, three existing marker interactions, five authored waves, one Guardian scene and one saved exit. Use a clearly identified temporary Guardian stand-in during testing; do not publish that stand-in as a new identity. Do not extend the current production save progression to doors five–eight until the whole chapter's checkpoint/ending rules are defined.

Acceptance: a first-time player can explain the objective; marker progress is visible; channeling never requires unavoidable damage; retrying preserves the current build; touch and reduced-motion users get the same warnings; the clue advances the mystery without contradicting Chapter I. Human sessions should measure objective confusion and trial length before expanding all four doors.
