# Ceiba Grove landmark atlas

Built-in image tool. Selected source: `landmarks-v1.png`, 1280×1280 RGBA with a two-by-two prop grid. Runtime assets: `assets/grove/`; slicing bounds, dimensions and hashes are in `assets/grove/manifest.json`. Ground texture is the unchanged Open Jungle Clearing image.

Prompt:

> Create one transparent 2D game environment prop sprite atlas for a Belizean jungle top-down action game. Square 1024x1024 canvas divided into four equal 512x512 cells, 2 columns by 2 rows. ONE isolated prop per cell with generous completely transparent gutters. TOP LEFT: broad low cluster of mossy limestone boulders with small jungle ferns, chunky silhouette, roughly 1.5:1 width to height. TOP RIGHT: fallen ceiba tree log lying horizontally, weathered bark, moss and a few tiny mushrooms, no tall branches, roughly 3:1 width to height. BOTTOM LEFT: short broken ancient limestone stone marker, irregular weathered stacked stones, moss, small roots, no lettering or symbols, roughly square silhouette. BOTTOM RIGHT: shallow rain puddle with a few pebbles and reeds on its edge, soft organic oval shape, transparent surrounding ground, no deep water, roughly 2:1 width to height. Visual style: polished hand-painted game art with chunky pixel-like texture matching colorful lime/olive rainforest grass and golden dirt, subtle dark outlines, warm limestone tones, deep green moss, readable at small sprite sizes. Camera: consistent elevated top-down three-quarter overhead view, suitable for a flat 2D arena, no perspective horizon. Soft light from upper left, short subtle grounding shadows included. Preserve exact 2x2 equal grid; every prop entirely contained in its own cell, at least 40 pixels of empty transparent margin all sides. No background terrain tiles, no characters, no borders, no labels, no text. Real transparent alpha background.

The tool returned a 1280-square atlas. Standard packing slices its four equal cells, trims transparent margins and exports each prop proportionally within 256×256. There is no creative repainting during packing.
