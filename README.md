## Progression and enemy balance — 0.6.1 (historical release)

Survival and Arena Run no longer stop XP levels, gems or XP powers at level 40. Every level costs `ceil(25 + 13 × (level − 1) + 0.5 × (level − 1)²)` XP, so later levels become progressively harder. All 24 stackable perks gain one maximum rank; seven unique perks stay at one. Once all useful choices are exhausted, repeatable Jungle Vitality adds five permanent max HP and heals 25, so even a full-health/maxed build still gains something on every level.

Existing weapon firing floors, two-ricochet limit, 180-degree/sec homing cap, sixteen Shock links and 96-active-enemy limit remain. Quick Hands ranks that cannot improve the normal firing interval provide +5% direct attack damage instead; temporary Badman does not affect that calculation. Cloud weapons do not receive this direct-only bonus and do not offer a firing rank with no benefit. Ricochet III adds +15% bounced-hit damage once, without adding a third bounce. Other additional ranks follow their existing formulas.

Alphas also gain 50% HP, applied multiplicatively: **1.725× previous alpha HP**, or **6× their newly increased normal family HP**. Damage, XP rewards, attack timing and the alpha schedule are unchanged. Story, classic, original RPG code and saves retain their existing rules.

## Critical Joe whole-body repair — 0.5.1

Root cause: 0.5.0 removed the integrated aim image's legs with destination-out polygons, then drew its torso over native walking legs. These independently scaled artworks have different poses, waist shapes and proportions. Between the eight aim directions, only the upper body was rotated, pulling the waist away from the legs. The special Sling path also clipped its original charge/release image at the waist before joining it to a different walking frame. A four-unit overlap did not solve incompatible silhouettes. The visible Sling-west gap was reproduced in Edge while moving at an intermediate aim angle. Previous exact-direction screenshots and geometry/hash checks did not establish body continuity.

Investigation: the native teenage-joe.png is 1402x1122 with twenty irregular crops, four rows and five poses per row, not uniform cells. Its crop metadata and prepared exports remain unchanged and valid. Native walking is selected from the two measured whole frames in each row; the hand patch is only a foreground grip overlay, not a body splice. Holding sources are illustrated direction boards exported to 96 separate complete 320x320 RGBA images, with per-image body heights and foot pivots. They are usable complete poses, but are not a production-ready walking/firing atlas or compatible torso/leg rig. Camera scaling was not the cause: the failure occurs with the same actor at the center and intermediate aim angles. Canvas save/restore remains balanced; the intentional body clipping and separate torso transform caused the cut.

Actual correction: the shared Joe renderer draws one complete native character for idle/walking, with the measured isolated weapon grip and original fist layered over its handle. While firing it draws one complete existing held-character image in the nearest of eight authored directions, with that image's own foot pivot and proportional body scale. The torso is never cut, replaced or rotated separately. Native rows retain their original crop dimensions instead of assuming equal cells. There is no body clipping, leg mask, destination-out pass or masked-body Canvas cache. Removed joe-motion.js, its generator, HTML loading and obsolete mask tests. Eliminated loading twelve unused masked-leg inputs. No PNG or source art was stretched, cropped again, edited or regenerated. All 227 runtime image hashes and 93 original source hashes still pass.

Movement speed, aim/projectile calculations, firing cooldowns, damage, collision, dash, weapons, enemies, maps, saves, Story and Survival logic are unchanged. The hotfix only changes shared Joe rendering and release/cache/test/documentation files.

Important art limitation: holding art contains complete static aim poses, not walking-while-firing cycles. During held fire Joe translates as one intact pose; his legs do not alternate through a fabricated layered cycle. Native walking resumes on release. - Space: dash in the movement direction, or toward the cursor when stationary. Dash gives brief protection and has a 1.65-second cooldown.
- Esc: pause/resume. Leaving the window or switching tabs pauses automatically; resume explicitly when you return.
- Mint experience gems: approach to collect. Level-ups pause the fight and offer three different upgrades; click one or press 1, 2, or 3.
- Survive ten waves. Tata Duende is the first boss in wave five; the original Legends of Belize Corrupted Jaguar Spirit is the second boss in wave ten. Restart is available after defeat, victory, or from pause.

Joe uses the supplied teenage sprite sheet: backwards cap, white vest, blue shorts, backpack, four-direction walking and slingshot poses. He begins every run with the slingshot. Defeating Tata Duende drops a visible staff at the boss's position. Walk over it to equip it: damage increases by 60%, firing becomes 15% faster, and every earned perk is retained. Wave six waits for the pickup; no inventory or extra action key is required. Restart removes the staff and restores the slingshot.

Crabs pursue slowly, mini jaguars rush quickly with low health, and shadow spirits maintain range and fire telegraphed projectiles. Tata Duende fires aimed spreads and radial patterns, speeding up below half health. The final Jaguar boss uses the original purple-and-green spirit artwork, telegraphs a fast charge with a dashed line, and fires wider volleys below half health. Edge arrivals are marked before enemies become active. The four limestone ruins block movement and shots; clear paths let enemies navigate around cover. A short grace period prevents contact from draining health every frame. Between waves, Joe regains 15 health and remaining gems gather automatically.

The ten waves contain 6, 9, 13, 18, 15, 20, 23, 26, 29 and 31 enemies, respectively: 190 enemies total, including both bosses. Later mobs gain health and speed while Joe gains perks and the staff.

## Prototype limits

- Keyboard/mouse and dual touch joysticks; no gamepad controls.
- One fixed arena and ten waves. Upgrades and the collected staff last for the current run; there is no saved progression.
- Balance is preliminary. Human playtesting and Safari/Firefox performance are not yet verified.
- Enemy steering is simple cover avoidance, not a general pathfinding system for larger maps.
- Folklore characters inspire the fiction; combat abilities are original game rules.
