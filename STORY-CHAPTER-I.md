# Official story direction — Chapter I: The Four Spirit Trials

Authoritative direction supplied by the creator on 18 October 2026. This consolidated record preserves the latest canon and dialogue; the earlier Story Structure Source remains historical.

Sixteen-year-old Joe Grind. Seven years after the original Legends of Belize. One mysterious obsidian stone. Four Guardian trials form a complete first mystery with a beginning, conflict, revelations and ending; eight further doors remain future story.

The central questions are why the obsidian brought Joe into the Spirit Arena, and what is happening to the Guardians who protect it. **The Hollow Keeper has never met Joe. It recognizes the obsidian stone, not Joe or his Staff.** Tata knows Joe; the Guardians do not all share that knowledge.

## Prologue — The Stone in the Forest

Joe lives with Grandma. She sends him for firewood. Beneath a tree’s roots, he finds black obsidian glowing purple. Touching it silences the forest. He brings it home despite Grandma’s warning. That night the stone glows; a distant voice calls. He awakens in an ancient jungle clearing, still carrying his Sling. “How I reach yah?” An echo answers: “The jungle remembers.” Wave 1 begins.

## The four trials

| Trial | Chapter waves | Guardian | Revelation / permanent reward |
|---|---|---|---|
| The Jungle Remembers | 1–5 | Tata Duende’s Manifestation | Tata remembers Joe’s earlier adventures; why did the stone wake now? Duende Staff |
| The Corrupted Underworld | 6–10 | The Hollow Keeper | The stone is a fragment of the broken Obsidian Heart; the Keeper’s memory of its breaking was taken. Obsidian Spirit Shard |
| Pine Ridge — The Jaguar’s Memory | 11–15 | Corrupted Jaguar Spirit | A familiar Guardian shares a vision of twelve symbols and a dark fracture; culprit unseen. Jaguar Spirit Claw |
| The Spirit Coast — The Last Tide | 16–20 | The Tide Warden | The arenas protect connected boundaries of the Spirit Layer; four doors restored, eight dark. Joe returns home |

Guardians survive purification. The Obsidian Heart is an original Spirit Arena concept associated with the Spirit Layer’s boundaries; its exact nature and who broke it remain unknown. The Jaguar’s exact identity is deliberately unresolved until the original RPG timeline is reconciled. This chapter does not undo the RPG’s restored-memory ending or identify its corruption as a confirmed return of the Forgetting.

## Arena direction and mechanics

- Jungle: Joe initially thinks it is a dream, but injuries hurt and the ruins seem to test him. Tata tests movement, accuracy, endurance and awareness with spirit projectiles. His hostile manifestation dissolves into green light.
- Underworld: obsidian formations, ancient limestone, roots and sick violet light. Destructible crystals empower nearby foes. Destroying them weakens enemies and briefly restores ground. The Keeper is limestone, roots and obsidian, overwhelmed by corruption; crystal volleys and marked fissures threaten Joe. Three protective crystals must break before its core takes damage.
- Pine Ridge: colder pine forest, limestone caves and waterfalls. Caves release corrupted creatures, and marked rockfalls threaten the clearing. The spectral Jaguar charges, summons bounded reinforcements through caves, accelerates below half HP and briefly hesitates with a trace of recognition.
- Coast: turquoise water, palms, fishing structures and unnatural tides. Rising water reduces safe space. Three drum circles provide recovery/protection. The Warden is water, shells and spirit light pulled apart by corruption: sweeping waves/projectiles, rising tide, then a barrier that requires all three drums between attacks. Damage alone cannot skip that objective.

## Dialogue and scenes

### A realm that feels sick

**Narrator:** Obsidian, ancient limestone and strange roots. Corruption spreads through the walls. Violet crystals strengthen nearby creatures. Shoot them to weaken the creatures and briefly restore the ground.

**Joe:** This da the same light from that rock…

### Who carries the broken fragment?

**A voice within the cavern:** Who carries the broken fragment?

**Joe:** Who deh?

**The voice:** That stone… where did you find it?

**Joe:** Eena the bush, near my grandma house!

**The voice:** Impossible.

**Narrator:** The cavern trembles. A corrupted Guardian is waiting. Destroy its protective crystals to expose its core.

### Pine Ridge · The Jaguar’s Memory

**Narrator:** Cold air among pines, waterfalls and limestone caves. The landscape is unstable. Rockfalls are marked before they strike. Creatures emerge through the caves.

**Joe:** Who broke the Heart?

### Something remembers

**Narrator:** A growl among the trees. Joe recognizes its sound from his younger adventures. A massive spectral Jaguar waits within a cave, its body covered in corrupted markings.

**Joe:** I know that sound…

### The Spirit Coast · The Last Tide

**Narrator:** Bright sand. Fishing structures. Turquoise water. The tide moves without wind. Three ancient drum circles glow. Stand inside for brief protection and recovery.

**A distant memory:** The doors must remain.

### The Guardians cannot hold forever

**A distant memory:** The Heart is fractured.

**Another memory:** The Guardians cannot hold forever.

**Narrator:** These arenas protect connected sections of the Spirit Layer. Corruption is weakening the barriers to the waking world.

**Narrator:** The Tide Warden is being pulled apart. When its barrier rises, stand in all three drum circles between attacks to break the corruption.

### The First Guardian

**Narrator:** The hostile manifestation dissolves into brilliant green spirit light. Tata Duende remains. Joe lowers his sling.

**Joe:** Finally! I thought you wah kill me!

**Tata:** Kill yu? Bwai, if dat was my intention, we wouldn’t be talking.

**Tata:** Seven years, and yu still finding trouble.

**Joe:** How you know about me?

**Narrator:** Tata looks toward the glowing mark on Joe’s hand.

**Tata:** More important… why that stone choose fi wake up now?

**Narrator:** Joe tries to ask another question, but Tata raises his staff.

**Tata:** One door open. Three remain.

**Reward:** The Duende Staff materializes. Permanently unlocked. A stone doorway opens, spilling purple light.

### The Broken Fragment

**Narrator:** The Keeper collapses. Darkness drains from its limestone, roots and obsidian. Its original consciousness returns.

**Hollow Keeper:** The darkness… it has loosened.

**Joe:** You know me?

**Hollow Keeper:** No, child. I have never seen you.

**Hollow Keeper:** But I know what you carry.

**Joe:** The stone?

**Hollow Keeper:** A fragment of the Obsidian Heart.

**Joe:** What happen to it?

**Hollow Keeper:** It was broken.

**Joe:** By who?

**Hollow Keeper:** That memory was taken from me.

**Narrator:** Someone—or something—has deliberately damaged the Guardians. The Heart’s precise nature remains unknown.

**Reward:** Obsidian Spirit Shard permanently unlocked.

**Hollow Keeper:** The next Guardian still remembers what I cannot.

**Narrator:** A doorway opens. Beyond it, wind moves through pine trees.

### Recognition

**Narrator:** The corrupted markings break. Joe approaches carefully, lowering his weapon. The restored Jaguar places its forehead against his palm.

**Joe:** I know you…

**Narrator:** A glowing claw-shaped mark appears. The Jaguar shares a memory.

### The Jaguar’s Memory

**Narrator:** The Obsidian Heart. Twelve glowing symbols surround it. A dark fracture spreads across its surface. The vision ends before Joe can see who caused it.

**Joe:** Twelve?

**Reward:** A spectral claw becomes the Jaguar Spirit Claw. Permanently unlocked.

**Narrator:** The Jaguar gives a final low growl. Joe smiles slightly.

**Joe:** Good fi see you too.

**Narrator:** The next doorway opens toward a bright coastal landscape.

### Four of Twelve

**Narrator:** The barrier breaks. Joe releases the corruption. The ocean becomes calm, and the Tide Warden returns to its original form.

**Tide Warden:** Four Guardians restored.

**Joe:** So… is that it?

**Tide Warden:** For this night.

**Joe:** What is this place?

**Tide Warden:** A boundary. A memory. A place between what lives and what remembers.

**Joe:** And the stone?

**Tide Warden:** A broken piece of the Heart that holds these doors apart.

**Joe:** Who broke it?

**Tide Warden:** That truth lies beyond the doors you have opened.

**Joe:** All this fighting, and nobody can tell me who doing this?

**Tide Warden:** You were brought here because the doors were failing. You have restored four.

### Not tonight

**Narrator:** Above the water, twelve symbols appear. Four shine brightly. Eight remain dark.

**Joe:** Eight more?

**Tide Warden:** Not tonight.

**Narrator:** Blinding light. Joe closes his eyes. The ocean fades into birds singing.

### Back at Grandma’s House

**Narrator:** Morning sunlight fills Joe’s room. Outside, chickens make noise.

**Grandma:** Joe! You wah sleep all day?

**Joe:** Just wah dream…

**Narrator:** The obsidian is still on the bedside table. Four marks glow faintly. He picks it up. For an instant, the Jaguar’s spirit mark appears on his hand. Joe freezes.

**Grandma:** Joe! Breakfast!

**Joe:** …Coming, Grandma.

**Narrator:** Joe slowly puts down the stone. Four marks pulse. Eight remain dark.

Underworld crystals have 65 HP during ordinary waves and 115 HP in the Keeper fight. Nearby ordinary enemies within 210 world units take 35% less damage and deal 25% more damage. Broken crystals leave an 8-second, 95-radius restoration zone healing 2 HP/second. Keeper HP 2100; three crystals seal its core. Both new Guardians have a 1.7-second arrival warning and explicit XP rewards.

Story Duende keeps 1050 HP, 20 contact damage and 13-damage bolts; the stronger Survival wave-20 Duende remains separate. Story Jaguar has 2400 HP, 22 contact damage, faster warned attacks below half health, cave adds capped at six concurrent, and rockfalls warned for 1.5 active seconds. The exact identity from Joe’s original adventure remains open.

Tide Warden has 3200 HP. Damage is clamped at the 65% and 30% phase boundaries, so a large hit cannot skip a phase. At 30%, all three drums must charge before further damage works. Standing within 38 units charges a drum in 1.2 seconds outside the attack windup. Drums also heal 8 HP and protect for 1.5 seconds on a 12-second cooldown. Tide expands gradually to 100 units inward; entering water deals damage through ordinary hurt/invulnerability rules. Warnings pause with the game.

Duende Staff, Obsidian Spirit Shard and Jaguar Spirit Claw unlock permanently in local Story storage; the Guardian scene equips its reward. Every new Story run starts with Sling. E / touch Pick Up cycles unlocked spirit weapons when no floor weapon is nearby. Story excludes the weapon-lock perk and Nuke, which would conflict with fixed rewards and mandatory objectives. Other perks and weapon behavior reuse the current shared engine.

Wave-start saves retain arena/wave, HP/build, XP, perk ranks, weapon enhancements and equipped weapon. Continue retries that wave, with encounter objectives reset. Purification scenes and open doorways save separately. The v2 format migrates supported v1 Jungle saves and preserves damaged, future-version or concurrently changed saves without overwriting them. Progress is local to this browser/device. Clearing browser storage removes it.

Survival is open during testing via SURVIVAL_TEST_OPEN in story-campaign.js. Chapter completion separately earns survivalUnlocked. Setting the switch false enforces the earned gate in both the menu and the direct Survival page through story-access.js.
